THE MACHINE CATALOGUE
======================================================================

Every home computer and console the register knows about: 304 machines
from 69 makers, 1975 to 1995. Filing a machine against one of these
fills in what is already known about that model, and asks only about
what varies between two of them -- the board issue, the case, the
region, the chips in the sockets.

Listed alphabetically by maker, and each maker's machines oldest
first. Where a machine was built by someone other than the maker it is
filed under, that is given in brackets. The catalogue itself is
api/app/machines.yaml; this list is generated from it by
tools/catalogue_list.py.
