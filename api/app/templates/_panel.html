{# A panel: one titled box, and the shape every section of an item page is drawn in. The pages grew a section at a time -- details, specs, the board, the parts, the files, the history -- and each was a heading with something under it, so what told you where one ended and the next began was a gap and a bold word. That works until a section is a list of things that themselves have parts and a gap inside them, and then the page is one long column of rules with nothing saying which level anything is at. A panel says it: the title sits on a band across the top, and what belongs to that section is inside the border. `meta` is the quiet half of the title -- a count, a date, whatever the section is measured in -- and is shown beside it rather than in it. Content goes in the panel's padded body by default. `flush` leaves it to place its own padding, which is what a section made of cards wants: their bands run the full width of the panel, edge to edge, the way a table's rows do. Such a section puts whatever else it has -- a toolbar, a form -- in a `pbody` of its own. `cls` is for a panel the stylesheet has something else to say about -- the QR code is hidden at the width where the phone reading it is already here. #} {% macro panel(title, meta='', flush=false, cls='') %}

{{ title }}

{% if flush %}{{ caller() }}{% else %}
{{ caller() }}
{% endif %}
{% endmacro %}
